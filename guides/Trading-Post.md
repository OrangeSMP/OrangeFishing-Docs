# Trading Post

> Based on OrangeFishing 1.0.0 defaults. Check the server’s menus for customized items, prices, and rewards.

Click the **Trading Post** NPC to open the Trading Post, then choose a shop:

| Shop | Pays with | Sells |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Armor & Tools** | Fishing coins | Weapons, tools and armor |
| **Furniture** | Crafting materials such as Tidal Fiber and Prismatic Dust | Decorations and furniture |

Each item has its own description and price. Material prices show how many of each material you have stored; check your pouch with `/fish materials`. The coin shop shows your coin balance at the top.

## Buying

**Click an item to buy it.** You pay the whole price and receive the item straight away.

- If you do not have enough coins or materials, nothing is taken and the chat tells you what the item costs.
- Make room in your inventory first. If it fills up before the item arrives, the price is returned.
- Clicking twice quickly buys only once; a second purchase waits until the first has gone through.
- On Bedrock, open an item's details and choose **Buy this item**.

Some items may need a rank or permission set by staff, and some may run a server reward instead of giving an item.

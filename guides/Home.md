# OrangeFishing Player Wiki

From quiet rivers to the lava pools of the Nether and the open void beneath End islands, OrangeFishing gives you three realms to explore, a collection to build, and a rod you can make your own. Catch unusual species, turn your haul into coins or crafting materials, compete in daily tournaments, and return throughout the year for seasonal fishing adventures.

**Start with `/fish` or `/fishing`.** Both commands open **Fishing Menu**, your fishing dashboard, and work interchangeably for every command in this guide. `/ofish` is another alias. OrangeFishing takes priority for `/fishing` when mcMMO is installed; use `/mcmmo:fishing` for mcMMO’s fishing skill page.

This guide describes version **1.0.0 with its default settings**. The server can change prices, rewards, recipes, dates, requirements, and available features. Check the live menus for the settings currently in use. Daily resets and seasonal dates default to **US/Eastern**, automatically following Eastern Standard Time and Eastern Daylight Time. Fish that require day, night, or particular weather follow the **Minecraft world's conditions**.

Java players browse Fishing through inventory menus. Bedrock players connecting through Geyser and Floodgate use native forms for every menu, including the dashboard, item lists, details, page navigation and confirmations. Select an entry to read its details, then choose the available action or go back. The same commands and gameplay rules apply to both clients. Sneak and right-click with a fishing rod to open your dashboard.

By default, configured menu labels, item names and message text use small capital letters, such as **ꜰɪѕʜɪɴɢ**. This guide uses regular letters for readability. Player names, numbers and usable commands retain their original text, and alphabetical lists keep their usual order. Server owners can turn this presentation off; it is separate from your personal chat settings.
